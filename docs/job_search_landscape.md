# Job Search Landscape — Executive Disability Insurance / Insurtech

**Profile:** VP/Director+ in disability insurance and group benefits, with engineering + product background. Targeting $260K+ base.

**Core insight:** ~90% of executive roles at this level are not posted publicly. Job boards are useful as radar, but recruiter relationships and networking drive most placements. Weight your effort accordingly.

---

## 2. Specialized Executive Job Boards

Worth checking weekly, not daily. Most charge a fee for full access.

- **BlueSteps** — ~$199–$399/year. Run by AESC (the executive recruiter trade association). Your profile becomes searchable to member recruiters. **Best paid investment for executive search visibility.**
- **ExecThread** — ~$60/month or invite-based free tier. Curated unposted opportunities sourced from members.
- **TheLadders** — free tier exists, premium ~$30–$70/month. Filter for $200K+ roles.
- **6FigureJobs** — free, decent for VP/Director listings
- **ExecuNet** — ~$50/month, includes networking events
- **Ivy Exec** — ~$40/month, executive job listings plus career resources
- **FT Executive Appointments** (ft.com/executive-appointments) — strong for international roles, free to browse
- **The Times Appointments** — UK-focused but covers global, free to browse

## Strategy Summary

Think of this as three concentric circles:

1. **Inner circle (highest leverage):** 5–8 executive recruiters you build real relationships with, especially Jacobson Group for the disability niche and Daversa/True Search for insurtech. Plus active engagement in DMEC.

2. **Middle circle:** Daily-monitored watchlist of 40–60 target company career pages plus 1–2 paid executive boards (BlueSteps is the top pick).

3. **Outer circle:** Industry-specific boards and LinkedIn job alerts for radar coverage.

Most placements at this level come from the inner circle. Posted-role applications are real but slow-moving — they work best when paired with a warm intro through the inner circle.
